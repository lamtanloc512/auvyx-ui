HTML structure

<article class="stat">
The card. Put several in an .auto-grid.
<header>
The label as a heading or <p>; an <svg> or .badge after it sits at the end.
<strong> or <data value="…">
The value, in tabular figures.
<p> Optional
A note. <span data-trend="up"> or "down" inside it colors a delta for a rise (--success) or a fall (--destructive). Whether a rise is good is yours to say: put data-trend="down" on a rise in refunds.

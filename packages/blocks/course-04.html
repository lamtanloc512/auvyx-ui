Description

A brand is more than a logo. In this course you'll build a complete identity for a real client brief, from the first interview to a brand book you can hand over with confidence.

We start with research: how to ask the right questions, collect references and turn them into a brief that keeps everyone aligned. Then we sketch, refine and test a logo, choose a palette and a type pairing, and design the touchpoints that make a brand feel consistent.

Every lesson ends with a short exercise, and the final project is a full brand book in your portfolio.

Who this course is for: beginners who want to work in branding, freelancers who want a repeatable process, and founders who want to understand what good branding involves.

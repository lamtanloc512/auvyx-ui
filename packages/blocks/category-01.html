About Home & Living

How do I care for linen?

Wash at 40°C, line dry and iron while slightly damp. Linen softens with every wash.

Do you deliver furniture?

Large items ship with a two-person crew to the room of your choice, free over $500.

Can I return home items?

Yes, unused items within 30 days. Textiles must be in their original packaging.
